# Grammar Snack 01 — Content Bank

## Contract

Topic: Present Simple vs Present Continuous. Level: B1, with explanations in clear A2 English. Each final-test variant has exactly 20 objective items in this order: `meaning` 1–4, `affirmative` 5–7, `negative` 8–10, `question` 11–14, `correction` 15–17, `context` 18–20. `answer` is the exact expected learner response; accept normal capitalization and terminal punctuation variants only.

### Variant 01

| # | type | prompt | answer | A2-English feedback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | meaning | Choose: My brother usually **(walks / is walking)** to college. | walks | Usually shows a habit, so use Present Simple. |
| 2 | meaning | Choose: Look! The bus **(comes / is coming)**. | is coming | Look! shows it is happening now. |
| 3 | meaning | Choose: I **(think / am thinking)** this bag is too expensive. | think | Think here means “have an opinion”. It is not an action now. |
| 4 | meaning | Choose: Why **(do you wear / are you wearing)** a coat today? It is hot. | are you wearing | Today is a temporary situation, so use Present Continuous. |
| 5 | affirmative | Complete: Sofia ___ (study) English every evening. | studies | A regular habit needs Present Simple. With she, add -ies. |
| 6 | affirmative | Complete: The chef ___ (make) lunch at the moment. | is making | At the moment means now. Make becomes making. |
| 7 | affirmative | Complete: He ___ (always / leave) his wet towel on the floor this week. | is always leaving | Always + Continuous can show an annoying temporary habit. |
| 8 | negative | Make negative: They are waiting for a taxi. | They are not waiting for a taxi. | Use are not before the -ing form. |
| 9 | negative | Make negative: Mia plays tennis on Mondays. | Mia does not play tennis on Mondays. | With does not, use the base verb: play. |
| 10 | negative | Complete: I ___ (not / understand) this instruction. | do not understand | Understand is usually a state, so use Present Simple. |
| 11 | question | Make a yes/no question: You are using my laptop. | Are you using my laptop? | Put are before the subject. |
| 12 | question | Make a yes/no question: Ben works in a bank. | Does Ben work in a bank? | Use Does + base verb for he/she/it. |
| 13 | question | Complete: What ___ you usually ___ (do) after school? | do; do | Use do + subject + base verb for routines. |
| 14 | question | Complete: Who ___ (cook) dinner tonight? | is cooking | Tonight here is a current arrangement, so use is cooking. |
| 15 | correction | Correct: She is knowing the answer. | She knows the answer. | Know is a state verb; use Present Simple. |
| 16 | correction | Correct: Does your parents live nearby? | Do your parents live nearby? | Parents is plural, so use Do. |
| 17 | correction | Correct: I am not usually drink coffee. | I do not usually drink coffee. | Usually is a habit; use do not + base verb. |
| 18 | context | Complete: My dad usually ___ (drive) to work, but today he ___ (take) the train. | drives; is taking | Usually = habit; today = temporary change. |
| 19 | context | Complete: We ___ (have) lunch at 1 every day, but we ___ (not have) lunch now. | have; are not having | Every day = routine; now = current action. |
| 20 | context | Complete: I ___ (not like) loud music, but I ___ (listen) to this song for my project. | do not like; am listening | Like is a state; listening is an action now. |
